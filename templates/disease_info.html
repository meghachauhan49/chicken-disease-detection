📚 Chicken Disease Information

Learn about common poultry diseases and general prevention measures.

🦠

Coccidiosis

Coccidiosis is a parasitic disease that affects the intestinal tract of chickens.

Common Signs

  • Diarrhea
  • Reduced appetite
  • Poor growth
  • Lethargy

Prevention

Maintain clean and dry litter, provide clean drinking water, and follow appropriate poultry management and veterinary guidance.

🐔

Healthy

A healthy chicken generally shows normal activity, feeding, growth and appearance.

Healthy Signs

  • Normal feeding and drinking
  • Active movement
  • Normal feathers
  • Normal droppings

Prevention

Maintain good hygiene, balanced nutrition, clean water, proper ventilation and regular monitoring.

⚠️

New Castle Disease

Newcastle disease is a contagious viral disease that can affect chickens and other birds.

Possible Signs

  • Respiratory problems
  • Reduced appetite
  • Drop in egg production
  • Neurological signs in some cases

Prevention

Good biosecurity, hygiene, isolation of affected birds and vaccination programs guided by a veterinarian are important.

🔬

Salmonella

Salmonella infection can affect poultry and may cause digestive and general health problems.

Possible Signs

  • Diarrhea
  • Weakness
  • Reduced appetite
  • Poor growth

Prevention

Keep housing clean, maintain good biosecurity, provide safe feed and water, and seek veterinary advice when illness is suspected.

Important: This information is for general awareness and prevention. A veterinarian should be consulted for diagnosis confirmation and treatment decisions.
